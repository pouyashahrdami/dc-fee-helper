How it works

From "how much?" to "I know" in three steps.

The panel appears on your Account Summary and does the arithmetic you used to do yourself.

1

Pick your program

Search every Durham College program, choose Domestic or International, and when your year of study starts.

2

Choose a semester

See the full breakdown: tuition, ancillary, student association, program and co-op fees.

3

See what's left to pay

Your account credit is subtracted for you, so the number you see is the number you need.

Privacy and trust

Your balance never leaves your browser.

A tool that sits on your student account should earn your trust. This one is open source, so you can read every line.

It reads

Your account balance from the page you're already looking at, only to work out what's left to pay.

It sends

Nothing about you. Its only request is for the college's public fee data: a program code, tuition type and term.

It stores

Your last selections and panel position, on your own device. No accounts, no analytics, no ads.

Install

Up and running in two minutes.

Works in Chrome, and in other Chromium browsers like Edge and Brave.

  1. Download and unzipGet the latest zip, unzip it, and keep the folder somewhere permanent.
  2. Open your extensions pageGo to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Load unpackedClick Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Open Account SummarySign in to MyCampus and open your Account Summary. The panel appears on the right.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this an official Durham College tool?

No. It's an independent, student-made project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Durham College. Numbers are estimates; the fees on your account are always the correct ones.

Is it safe to use on my student account?

It never asks for your password and never sends your balance anywhere. It reads the balance already shown on the page and fetches public fee data. The code is open for anyone to inspect.

Why do I have to turn on Developer mode?

The extension isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so it's installed from the downloaded folder. It does exactly the same thing either way.

Does it work for international students and co-op programs?

Yes. Pick International in the panel, and co-op fees appear as their own line when a semester has one.

The numbers look wrong. What now?

Check them against the official estimator using the button in the panel. If they still differ, open an issue. Please never post your real balance or student details.
