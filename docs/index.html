DC Fee Helper

Know what you owe before you register.

A free Chrome extension for Durham College students. Your semester fees and your account balance, side by side on your MyCampus Account Summary.

Download (free) View on GitHub
The panel on the Account Summary page (demo numbers).

What it does

Search once, remembered

Pick your program, Domestic or International, and when your year starts. It's saved for next time.

Credit applied for you

See the semester fee breakdown and exactly what's still to pay after your account credit.

One click to the source

Open the official estimator with your selections already filled in.

Private by design

Your balance never leaves your browser. No accounts, no tracking, open source.

Install in 2 minutes

  1. Download the latest zip and unzip it. Keep the folder somewhere permanent.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Sign in to MyCampus and open Account Summary. The panel appears on the right.

Works in Chrome, Edge and Brave. Prefer to have an AI do the setup or change the colors? The README has copy-paste prompts.

Good to know

  • Unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Durham College. Numbers are estimates; the fees on your account are always correct.
  • Fees are full-time estimates from the college's public estimator data. Part-time loads can differ.
  • Numbers look wrong? Open an issue (never post your real balance).
